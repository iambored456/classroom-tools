# Paper Airplane Visualizer

Interactive Basic Dart, Arrowhead and Raven folding lessons with a continuous 3D model of a Letter-sized sheet. Select the airplane profile in the header to open the design picker. Basic Dart follows five stages and eight labelled folds; Arrowhead follows ten stages, thirteen labelled folds and a nose-pocket tuck; Raven follows eight stages and twelve labelled folds. Each lesson follows its supplied Fold'NFly guide. Instructions are paraphrased and geometry is independently implemented; source PDFs are not redistributed. The original guides are credited and linked inside the app.

Run `pnpm --filter paper-airplane-visualizer dev` and open `http://localhost:5193/paper-airplane-visualizer/`, or use the Classroom Tools launcher and hub. Build with `pnpm --filter paper-airplane-visualizer build`; use `BASE_URL=/classroom-tools/paper-airplane-visualizer/` for GitHub Pages. The repository's Pages pipeline discovers the app automatically.

Each selected fold loops forward and backward with endpoint holds. The five main steps contain eight labelled substeps: 1V, 2V, 3V, 4M, 5M, 6V, 7V and 8V. Select a label beneath its step or use Next fold / Previous fold to move through the sequence. Paired corners, nose folds and wings animate individually while previous folds remain complete. Selecting a main step starts its first substep. The scrubber pauses playback; Play resumes in the appropriate direction. Changing substeps animates through intermediate states. Step 1 includes making and reopening the centre crease. Reduced-motion preferences pause initial playback. The original pattern uses 45-degree corner creases, 22.5-degree narrowing creases and wing hinges to the quarter-width tail positions.

Drag to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom; right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. Reset view restores framing without changing the fold. Focus the 3D view to rotate with arrow keys. Space plays/pauses, 1–5 chooses a step, +/− zooms, and R resets the view. Fullscreen is available where supported. No account or server data is needed.

The pure model in `src/model.js` clips a full sheet into convex panels, preserving original coordinates and a fold history. Animated hinge rotations preserve panel lengths and surface area. Wings rotate in their half's coordinate frame. Both body halves move symmetrically for visibility. Rendering gives the paper a 0.008-inch thickness with front, back and edge surfaces. Hinges sit at the surface of the current layer stack so folded corners settle above the receiving sheet. The ideal attached-sheet model remains available for geometry checks; this is not a simulation of elasticity or flight.

Once a fold has been completed, its crease stays visible as a fine gray line when reopened or scrubbed backward. These marks follow both sides of the paper and remain depth-occluded by other layers. Reloading starts with a fresh sheet.

Printed guides are always on. They recreate the PDF's blue dashed valley lines, red mountain lines, and numbered V/M labels in corresponding positions on the front and reverse. The markings are mapped to the original sheet coordinates so they move with every fold. View other side smoothly orbits the camera through a half-turn in 0.9 seconds, preserving zoom and elevation. Dragging, zooming or resetting the camera interrupts the turn. Reduced-motion preferences use an immediate change. The transparent controller uses enlarged text; controls help and the original guide link are available from the question-mark button.

Arrowhead has its own panels, hinges, layer offsets and front/reverse markings. The paper is split at each crease in its current folded position so the top hem and nose folds carry their existing layers with them. The pocket tuck uses a simplified flex animation to open the nose layers and seat the loose point; it is an instructional illustration, not a cloth simulation. The selected design is remembered locally. Number keys select main steps (0 selects step 10); the longer Arrowhead step strip scrolls as you advance.

Raven has its own crease measurements and printed markings, starting with the blue side facing the viewer. The two sets of corner folds animate individually. Its 9V locking tab lifts the folded nose layers over the corner flaps while the original tail stays in place, followed by the body and two swept wings.

`pnpm --filter paper-airplane-visualizer test` checks all three models' sheet area, panel rigidity, attached ideal seams and final symmetry, plus Basic Dart's crease classification and layer contact. Arrowhead's illustrated pocket flex is excluded from rigid-panel checks. Raven also checks each paired fold and its locking tab independently. `window.__SCREENSHOT__` selects a paused final Basic Dart for the repository's preview capture pipeline.
